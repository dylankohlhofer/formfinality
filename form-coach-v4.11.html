Make yourself comfortable

  
Prop the camera securely. Start with a view of the movement, then follow the framing hints. There is no single correct distance for every phone or body.

  
What the controls mean

  
  • Show me how: watch the movement demonstration.
  • Guide outline: a reference drawing, not a size or shape you must match.
    • 
  
  • Pause: take a break without losing observed work. P is the keyboard shortcut.
    • 
  
  • Skip: move on without a form verdict for this set. S is the shortcut.
    • 
  
  • Follow along: finish manually with teaching but no camera counting or form assessment.

  
When counting pauses
A missing joint or unsuitable view is not a bad-form score. Read the on-screen explanation, reposition if convenient, or choose Follow along. Stop if a movement hurts.

  
Private by design
Camera video and speech processing stay on this device. The browser build downloads its model and recorded audio when needed; it is not a guaranteed offline installation. Diagnostics are optional, local and only exported by you.

  
Device interruption pauses a workout. Return and choose Resume; time away is never exercise credit. Progress is kept in memory for this visit, not saved across reloads.
